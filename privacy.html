Privacy and terms

Short, because there is not much to say.

World of Trade is a free educational app about physical commodity trading. It is run by [NOME E INDIRIZZO DEL TITOLARE], who is the data controller. Questions or requests: [INDIRIZZO EMAIL DI CONTATTO].

What is stored, and why

Only two things:

  • Your email address and password. They identify your account so your progress can follow you to another device. Authentication is handled by Supabase; the password is hashed by them and is never visible to this app or to us.
  • Your progress. Which lessons you completed, experience points, accuracy per lesson, badges, day streak, answer streak, and which exercises you got wrong so they can be brought back for review.

That is the complete list. No name, no company, no payment details, no location, no device fingerprint.

What is not done

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no profiling.
  • No cookies are set for tracking. The app uses your browser's local storage to keep your progress on the device and to remember that you are signed in.
  • Your data is never sold, and never shared with anyone except the processor named below.

Where it is stored

The data is processed to perform the service you asked for when creating the account. It is kept until you ask for the account to be deleted, or until the service is shut down.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction, or its deletion, at the contact address above. Deleting the account removes the progress record with it. If you are in the EU or Switzerland you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

You can avoid all of this by not creating an account, in which case nothing leaves your browser.

Everything here is written to teach the mechanics of physical commodity trading. It is not trading, investment, legal, tax or accounting advice, and it is not a recommendation to trade anything.

Prices, cargoes, counterparties and calculations in the exercises are illustrative and simplified for teaching. They are not market data and they do not model any real market. Real contracts are governed by their own wording, the applicable charterparty and the applicable law, and these differ from the general cases described here.
