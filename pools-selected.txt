10	choice	What does origination actually add to a trading business?	WHY: Anyone can trade the visible market. Origination creates the flow that others cannot reach.
11	choice	Why is a long-term offtake contract valuable beyond its margin?	WHY: Structural flow is what turns a trading desk into a business rather than a series of bets.
12	choice	A prepayment finances a producer against future deliveries. What is the core risk?	WHY: Structured prepayments are credit deals dressed as trades, and they should be underwritten as credit.
13	choice	Why does a force majeure clause deserve careful reading?	WHY: The events listed, the notice requirements and the consequences vary enormously between contracts.
14	choice	What does a change-of-law clause protect against?	WHY: In commodities, regulatory change is a routine commercial risk, not an exotic one.
15	choice	Why does governing law and arbitration seat matter commercially?	WHY: A right you cannot enforce against assets you can reach is not worth what it appears to be.
16	choice	A counterparty in a sanctioned jurisdiction offers an attractive deal. What is the correct first step?	WHY: Sanctions exposure can end a company, not just a trade, and it cannot be priced away.
17	choice	What is the practical value of a parent company guarantee?	WHY: Many trading counterparties are small entities. Who stands behind them is the real credit question.
18	choice	Why do desks avoid conflicting standard terms in a contract chain?	WHY: Back-to-back means the terms match, not just the volumes and dates.
19	numeric	You prepaid $7,000,000 against 7 equal monthly deliveries. 3 have been delivered. How much is still at risk?	WHY: $7,000,000 × (1 − 3/7) = $4,000,000 still unsecured. A prepayment is credit exposure that unwinds only as cargo arrives.
20	numeric	A take-or-pay contract sets a minimum of 80,000 t. You lift 65,000 t. At $16/t, what do you owe on the shortfall?	WHY: (80,000 − 65,000) × 16 = $240,000. Take-or-pay turns an optimistic forecast into a real bill.
21	numeric	An offtake runs 7 years for 30,000 t a year at $9/t. Total contribution over the life of the contract?	WHY: 30,000 × 9 × 7 = $1,890,000. This is why origination is valued differently from a spot trade.
